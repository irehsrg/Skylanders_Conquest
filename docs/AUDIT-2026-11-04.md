# Polish pass — 2026-11-04

One session, eight commits, every one gated on a headless match passing with
zero logged errors. The goal was the gap between "prototype" and "one match
that feels like a game": feedback, sound, items, AI, HUD, a fourth god, and a
way to prove none of it broke anything.

## The harness first

`USkylandersSmokeTestSubsystem` (`-SkySmoke=<seconds>`) runs the Joust map
under `-nullrhi`, drives the player with `ASkylandersCharacter::DriveAsBot`
(levels abilities, buys a starter, walks with its wave on the navmesh, shoots,
casts, retreats under 30% health, respawns), samples the match every second,
counts named events (`SKY_COUNT`), captures Error-level log lines and writes
`Saved/SmokeTest/report.json`. `Scripts/smoke_test.py` wraps it, including
`--character` and `--all-characters`.

Invariants for a 120 s run: full waves spawn, minions die, gods take damage,
the player shoots, casts, takes damage, shakes the camera and plays sounds,
gods and the player buy items, AI state changes stay under 200 in total, no
errors. Final state: all four kits pass.

## What changed, in order

1. **Hit feedback.** Additive overlay flash on every damaged actor
   (`SkylandersHitFlash`, `/Game/VFX/M_HitFlash`), wave-oscillator camera
   shakes (light on damage and casts, heavy on ults and death).
2. **Sound.** Twelve placeholder cues synthesized by
   `Scripts/gen_placeholder_sfx.py` (stdlib only, deterministic), imported
   headless. `SkylandersSoundLibrary` fills every null sound slot, and hits,
   kills, recall, respawn, ults and shop clicks play where nothing did.
3. **Announcer.** Center banner with a queue: 3-2-1-FIGHT, FIRST BLOOD, god
   slain, you were slain, tower and phoenix destroyed, titan vulnerable; a
   persistent status line for recall and respawn countdowns. Every
   `AddOnScreenDebugMessage` in gameplay code is gone.
4. **Items.** `docs/ITEMS-DESIGN.md`: 36 items, cooldown reduction (capped
   40%) and penetration (flat, floored), 10 base protections + 1 per level so
   penetration matters, archetype purchase lists for AI gods, 500 starting
   gold. Fixed: the shop bound Buy buttons only for items 1-12, so 13-20 were
   never purchasable.
5. **HUD cost and assists.** HUD writes once per frame with cached property
   lookups; per-frame logs demoted to Verbose (58,265 LogTemp lines per match
   became 143). Assists with a 10 s window, kill and death counters on gods, a
   two-team scoreboard, respawn timers that scale with match time.
6. **C++ main HUD.** `USkylandersMainHUDWidget` replaces the Blueprint HUD:
   portrait and level badge, outlined health/mana numbers, XP bar, ability
   slots with cooldown shade and rank pips, item row, gold counter, one
   palette. The Blueprint still works if assigned explicitly.
7. **AI.** `SetState` with counters; `TowerBackoff` state with a 4 s hold and
   a cover requirement, separate from low-health `Retreating`; `Jungling`
   state that takes buff camps (camps now fight gods and grant them the buff);
   allies join the player's fights; targeting prefers the lowest-health hero
   and holds a target 1.5 s.
8. **Spyro.** All 25 rip animations imported through the legacy FBX path
   (Interchange raises on the rip's extra bone tracks). Kit: Flameball,
   Flame Breath, Charge, Comet Dash, Daybringer Flame. Select-screen tile,
   `-SkyCharacter=` flag, and the blue ally roster excludes the player's pick.

Cleanup: the vendored, disabled VisualStudioTools plugin is gone;
`DefaultEngine.ini` duplicates removed.

## Found along the way

- The editor crashed on startup because auto-reimport scanned the 4.7 GB
  asset-rip clone that lives under `Content/Skylanders-Models/` and hit a bad
  FBX. Content directory monitoring is now off in the per-user editor
  settings (`Saved/Config/WindowsEditor/EditorPerProjectUserSettings.ini`,
  backup beside it). The clone is gitignored and the scripts expect it there.
- God passive gold scaled with level without a cap (2x the player by 20
  minutes); capped at level 6.
- The navmesh path into the center Damage Buff room dead-ends; gods reach it
  only through the straight-steer fallback. Worth checking in the editor.
- A 180 s invariant on god purchases was flaky because gods only shop at
  base; starting gold made it deterministic.
