# Code Audit — 2026-08-02 (3v3 combat pass)

Follow-up to `AUDIT-2026-07-03.md`, focused on what Tier 2's "teams of three"
change left half-wired. Every fix here is C++ only — no editor or asset work.

**Not compiled.** This pass was written without an engine install, so nothing
below has been through UBT. Build before playtesting.

## Fixed

### Critical
- **Gods could not auto-attack each other.** `ASkylandersEnemyGod::PerformAttack`
  branched on player / minion / tower / titan and fell off the end for an
  `ASkylandersEnemyGod` target. Every god-vs-god basic attack — the entire
  ally-vs-enemy duel — dealt zero damage, silently. Only the 8-second burst
  ability (which does handle gods) was landing. Added the god branch, with the
  same "the target applies its own protections" rule and lifesteal handling the
  player branch uses.

- **Minions ignored gods entirely.** `ASkylandersMinion::FindTarget` considered
  enemy minions and the human player, nothing else, so both waves walked through
  the AI gods as if they weren't on the map — and blue minions had no hero to
  target at all. Target selection now picks the nearest *opposing hero*, where a
  hero is the player (who fights for `Friendly`) or any god whose `Team` differs.
  Added the matching damage branch in `AttackTarget` and generalized forced aggro
  so a god poking the wave pulls it the way the player does. The new
  `IsOpposingHero()` helper is the single place team-vs-hero logic lives.

### High
- **Ally gods farmed gold and creep score into the player's account.**
  `ASkylandersMinion::Die` paid the player for *any* red minion death regardless
  of who killed it, and AI gods earned nothing from farming — so a 3v3 fed the
  player their teammates' entire lane income while the ally gods stayed broke and
  never finished item builds. Minions now track `LastDamageCauser`; a god last-hit
  pays that god, and anything else still falls back to paying the player (red
  minions only ever die to the player's side, so this keeps the old feel for
  wave/tower kills rather than switching to strict last-hit rules).

- **Same problem for god kills.** `ASkylandersEnemyGod::Die` gave the player XP,
  gold, and a `Kills++` for every red god death anywhere on the map. An ally god
  winning a duel across the map inflated the player's KDA and paid them ~150+
  gold. Kills are now credited to the god that landed the blow (with a kill-feed
  line naming them); the player is only paid when no god did it.

### Medium
- **Gods never walked toward the wave.** `FindNearestEnemyMinion` capped its
  search at `AttackRange`, so the laning "approach the minion" branch was
  unreachable — the god only ever reacted to minions already on top of it, and
  otherwise marched past a live wave straight at the structures. The search now
  takes a radius; laning passes `AggroRange`, and the last-hit-during-a-fight
  call keeps the old `AttackRange` default.

- **Ranged minions could not chase.** They get `AttackRange` 600 but inherited
  `AggroRange` 500, making the chase branch in `UpdateAI` dead code (flagged in
  the July audit, left open). `AggroRange` is now 700 for ranged.

- **Minion chip damage yanked gods out of lane.** Now that minions attack gods,
  the "damaged while Laning -> switch to Fighting" rule fired on every wave tick.
  The state flip is restricted to hits from an opposing *hero*, and it tests the
  resolved attacking pawn rather than the raw damage causer (previously a
  projectile's own location was what `WouldChaseIntoEnemyTower` checked).

- **Machine-gun loop anim froze on frame 0** for characters running the
  code-driven `USkylandersSimpleAnimInstance` (Hex, Tree Rex). The loop chain
  asked `Montage_IsPlaying`, which is always false there because that instance
  never creates montages, so the loop restarted every tick. Added
  `IsFullBodyAnimPlaying()` and branch on the instance type.

### Low
- God damage aggro notified `ETowerTeam::Enemy` towers and minions by name rather
  than the damaged god's own team. Correct for red gods by luck; wrong the moment
  anything hits a blue ally. Now uses `Team`.

## Playtest checklist

1. Stand back and watch a lane fight — ally and enemy gods should trade
   auto-attack beams and actually lose health, not just trade 8-second bursts.
2. Both minion waves should stop and hit gods that walk into them.
3. Ranged minions should walk toward a target 600-700 units out.
4. Let an ally god farm a wave: their gold should climb (they should start
   completing items), and your creep score should *not*.
5. Kill feed should read "<Ally> slew <Enemy>" when a teammate gets a kill, and
   your KDA shouldn't move for it.
6. Gods should walk up to the wave and last-hit instead of marching past it.

## Still open

- The July audit's note that `MachineGunLoopAnim` is never played is stale — the
  chain exists; only the anim-instance branch above was missing.
- Target selection still runs `GetAllActorsOfClass` per minion per tick (now four
  calls). Consistent with the existing code, but it's the obvious first target
  when Tier 4 performance work starts.
- Kill credit is "last hit, else the player." No assist gold, no shared XP.
